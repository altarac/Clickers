Every student answers by holding up a piece of paper.

Each student gets one printed card with a square black-and-white marker on it. The four edges are labelled A, B, C and D. To answer, they rotate the card so their choice is at the top and hold it up. You sweep the room with a camera; Clickers reads every card at once and tallies the answers. No logins, no phones for students, no batteries.

Print one card per student

Print cards 1–30 once and reuse them all year — they are not tied to a name or a class. Laminate them if you can. Set your printer to landscape at 100% scale; each card prints a 50 mm ruler so you can confirm nothing got shrunk.

2

Set up a question

Type a question into the Question Bank, run an unscripted Quick Poll with no set-up at all, or upload a PDF you already teach from and drop draggable A/B/C/D choices straight onto the page.

3

Project it and scan the room

Open the projector window on your second screen, then point your laptop webcam — or your phone, linked by QR code — at the class. Cards are recognised as students raise them, and the checklist fills in without revealing anyone’s answer.

4

Reveal the results

When everyone has answered, hit Project Results. The bar chart appears on the projector with the correct answer highlighted. Reset and ask the next question without leaving the screen.

First five minutes

  • Print cards 1–5 as a test. Hold one up two metres from your webcam and check it is detected before you print thirty.
  • Run a Quick Poll — it needs no question written in advance. Ask anything out loud and let the class answer A–D.
  • Open the projector window and drag it to your second display, then set it full screen.
  • Link your phone with the QR code in the sidebar if your laptop camera cannot see the back row. The phone becomes a roaming scanner.

Common questions

Can students see each other’s answers?
Not easily — the marker looks the same from across the room whichever way it is turned. Private mode is on by default, so your own screen shows only a tick, never the letter.
A card is not being detected.
Almost always print scale or glare. Re-print at 100% (check the 50 mm ruler on the card), keep the white border around the marker clean, and avoid direct light on laminated cards.
Do I need to install anything?
No, for the laptop camera. To use a phone as the scanner on the same Wi-Fi, run python3 server.py from the project folder; otherwise host the file and the phone will relay over the internet.
Where is my data stored?
In this browser only. Questions and imported PDF pages stay on this machine — use Save Set in the Question Bank to export a file you can carry to another computer.

Print Clicker Cards

Cards are generic and reusable — card #7 is card #7 in every class, every year. Print once, laminate, and hand them out. Each card prints with the marker on one half and the student-facing instructions on the other, so you can fold it down the dashed line.

30 Cards
80 mm

Size of the black square itself. An 80 mm marker is read comfortably from about 3 m by a 720p webcam — detection depends on how many camera pixels the square covers, so distance matters far more than lighting. For a room deeper than that, walk the phone scanner up the aisles rather than printing larger; one card per page is already close to the width of the paper.

Before you print: in the browser print dialog set Layout: Landscape, Margins: Default and Scale: 100% (not “Fit to page”). Scaling changes the physical marker size and the ruler printed on each card will tell you if it happened.
